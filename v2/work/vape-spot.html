Vape Spot

A Shopify store for a vape shop: the theme, the homepage, the product pages and the photos.

Problem
The live theme loaded scripts no page used, and the homepage opened on a photo of the shop front, with products below it.
What I did
I audited the theme, cut what was loading for nothing, rebuilt the homepage around categories and put prices and flavours on every product card.
Result
Products, prices and flavours show from the first screen, and every listing image follows one look.
Client:Vape Spot
My Role:Design and front-end, Shopify
Year:2026
Service Provided:Research, brand to product, shipped with AI

Audit

The live theme was loading things no page used.

I pulled the live theme into Git as a baseline and went through what every page loaded before changing anything.

A slider nobody saw

Revolution Slider assets rendered on zero pages but loaded on every page. Switched off.

Someone else’s script

A duplicate instant.page script was loading from an unrelated store’s CDN. Removed.

Oversized images

Brand logos were 480px files squeezed into 131px slots. Images are now sized to their slot, lazy below the fold, and the first row loads straight away.

Before and after

A homepage built around categories.

The old homepage opened on a photo of the shop front. I rebuilt it around a category grid with custom icons, a compact header, a proper tablet layout and a swipeable reviews row.

  2. A full-width photo of the shop front came first. Products started below it.Shop by category comes first, as a numbered grid with custom icons.
  3. Categories were a row of small grey icons in the header.Each category gets a large tile, and the grid has a proper tablet layout.
  4. The top bar said some products might not be online and asked people to call.The top bar carries the offers: a free gift over $50, 10% cash back, free delivery over $100.

Before and after

A collection that shows the products.

Disposables used to open on brand tiles: a black silhouette and a name, no prices. Now it lists the products, each with its price, any deal and how many flavours it comes in.

  1. BeforeAfter
  2. Brand tiles with no prices. Shoppers had to click through to see a product.Product cards with the price, any multi-buy deal and a flavour count.
  3. A theme demo placeholder, “400x350px”, was live in the grid.Every listing image follows one look: the product in a burst of fruit on white.

Before: vapespot.ca/collections/disposables on 9 August 2025, rendered from the Internet Archive’s capture. After: November 2026.

Product cards

Flavours on the card, not three clicks away.

Most products come in a long list of flavours. The cards now show them as chips, so shoppers see every flavour before they open a product.

Offers

Three offers, always in view.

A blue marquee carries the store’s offers: a free gift on orders over $50, 10% cash back when you sign up, and free delivery over $100. Multi-buy deals show the old price struck through.

Brand to product

The shop’s own photos, cleaned up.

I polished the store’s photos for its Google Business Profile and the site’s banners, so the shop people find on Google looks like the one they walk into.

Listings

One look for every product image.

Each listing image puts the product in a burst of fruit on white, so the whole grid reads as one store.
