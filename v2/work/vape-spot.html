Vape Spot

A redesign of a vape shop’s Shopify store, built to the owner’s brief: keep it minimal.

Problem
The owner wanted a minimal store, but the homepage opened on a photo of the shop front, collections hid their prices, and the theme loaded scripts no page used.
What I did
I audited the theme first, then redesigned the homepage, collections and product cards to the brief: less on the page, products and prices first.
Result
Products, prices and flavours show from the first screen, and every listing image follows one look.
Client:Vape Spot
My Role:Design and front-end, Shopify
Year:2026
Service Provided:Research, brand to product, shipped with AI

01The brief

The owner wanted it minimal.

This was a redesign of the store’s Shopify site, and every decision went back to that brief.

The old store made people work to find a product.

Homepage

  • A full-width photo of the shop front came first. Products started below it.
  • Categories were a row of small grey icons in the header.
  • The top bar said some products might not be online and asked people to call.

Collections

  • Brand tiles with no prices, so shoppers had to click through to see a product.
  • Flavours were hidden inside each product page.
  • A theme demo placeholder, “400x350px”, was live in the grid.
02The audit

Before changing anything, I checked what every page loaded.

I pulled the live theme into Git as a baseline, then cut what was loading for nothing.

A slider nobody saw

Revolution Slider assets loaded on every page and showed on none. Switched off.

Someone else’s script

A duplicate instant.page script loaded from an unrelated store’s CDN. Removed.

Oversized images

Brand logos were 480px files in 131px slots. Images are now sized to their slot.

03The redesign

Products first, the shop front second.

Shop by category opens the homepage, and the top bar carries the offers instead of a warning.

Before: vapespot.ca on 16 January 2026, from the Internet Archive. After: vapespot.ca, November 2026.

Prices on the grid, not behind a click.

Collections list the products, each with its price, any deal and how many flavours it comes in.

Before: vapespot.ca/collections/disposables on 9 August 2025, from the Internet Archive. After: November 2026.

Every flavour on the card.

Flavours open as chips under each product, so shoppers see them all before they click.

Three offers, always in view.

A free gift over $50, 10% cash back on sign-up, and free delivery over $100. Multi-buy deals strike through the old price.

04The look

The shop people find on Google looks like the one they walk into.

I cleaned up the store’s own photos for its Google Business Profile and the site’s banners.

One look for every product image.

The product in a burst of fruit on white, so the whole grid reads as one store.
