CredNest.Money helpfor students.

A fintech app concept for international students in Canada: budget, track spending, and finally understand credit cards and credit scores.

Role
UX designer
Context
Langara College team project
Tools
Figma, Illustrator, Photoshop
Process
Discover, empathise, define, ideate, prototype, test

The problem.

International students often struggle to manage their money in a new country, especially credit cards.

Budgeting, tracking expenses and understanding credit scores are all hard when you're new. CredNest set out to fix the lack of simple tools and know-how for managing money and credit as a newcomer.

What students told us.

We ran surveys and in-depth interviews using the Critical Incident Technique, focusing on specific moments that shaped how they handle money. Five feelings kept coming up.

Fear

Stress when looking at their total expenses.

Confusion

About credit score maths and credit card terms.

Overwhelm

From juggling several accounts at once.

Insecurity

About making a money mistake, especially with credit.

Curiosity

A real wish to learn how it all works.

From notes to patterns.

An affinity diagram sorted the interview notes into interests, pain points, motivations, goals, needs and influences. That became two personas: a graduate who needs to understand credit cards in Canada, and an undergraduate who wants to track spending and see how it hits his credit score.

Structure before screens.

The information architecture splits the app into three jobs: manage cards, see the dashboard, and view analytics. Low-fi wireframes came next and went in front of users before any visual design.

The look.

A deep green palette, Montserrat for headings and Noto Sans for body text, used across every screen.
