audit.

The live theme was loading things no page used.

I pulled the live theme into Git as a baseline and went through what every page loaded before changing anything.

A slider nobody saw

Revolution Slider assets rendered on zero pages but loaded on every page. Switched off.

Someone else's script

A duplicate instant.page script was loading from an unrelated store's CDN. Removed.

Oversized images

Brand logos were 480px files squeezed into 131px slots. Images are now sized to their slot, lazy below the fold, and the first row loads straight away.

homepage.

A homepage built around categories.

I rebuilt the homepage around a category grid with custom icons, a compact header, a proper tablet layout and a swipeable reviews row.

product cards.

Flavours on the card, not three clicks away.

Most products come in a long list of flavours. The cards now show them as chips, so shoppers see every flavour before they open a product.

offers.

Three offers, always in view.

A blue marquee carries the store's offers: a free gift on orders over $50, 10% cash back when you sign up, and free delivery over $100. Multi-buy deals show the old price struck through.

brand to product.

The shop's own photos, cleaned up.

I polished the store's photos for its Google Business Profile and the site's banners, so the shop people find on Google looks like the one they walk into.

listings.

One look for every product image.

Each listing image puts the product in a burst of fruit on white, so the whole grid reads as one store.
